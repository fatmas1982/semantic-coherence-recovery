# Reviewer 1 — Information Science / Information Quality revision log

Target: Information Processing & Management (IP&M)
Date: 2026-09-19

## What was changed

1. **Information-quality theory anchored explicitly.** Added Section 1.2.1 linking the bounded three-axis construct to established information-quality theory (Wang & Strong, 1996; Lee et al., 2002/AIMQ). The manuscript now states explicitly that it measures a subset of information quality rather than a universal information-quality ontology.
2. **Concept hierarchy made explicit.** Added a conceptual figure mapping generated-information quality to (a) linguistic mechanism, (b) grounding status, and (c) functional severity, while listing dimensions outside scope.
3. **Information-science novelty strengthened.** The manuscript now states that its information-science contribution is a validity workflow in which the automatic evaluator is treated as a measurement system whose credibility depends on reference provenance, shortcut exposure, evidence, and decision protocol.
4. **Use boundary clarified.** Section 3.1 now distinguishes supported uses (diagnostic measurement, within-corpus recovery, evaluator-validity auditing) from unsupported uses (natural prevalence, universal IQ score, end-user perceived IQ, architecture ranking, deployment validation).
5. **Information-quality management implications added.** Section 7.7 now gives five operational safeguards: validate the reference; preserve disagreement; audit provenance/shortcuts; stress-test judge protocol; and use selective human review near narrow boundaries.
6. **Adjudication-instability exclusion sensitivity executed.** The 29 targeted same-adjudicator re-review cases were excluded from frozen M3 Condition-C and archived M4 P1/identity scoring without retraining or relabelling.
7. **Limitations updated.** The manuscript explicitly states the bounded IQ dimensions, the absence of independent second-adjudicator replication, and what the exclusion sensitivity does and does not establish.
8. **Supplement updated.** The exclusion-sensitivity results and interpretation boundary were added to S9.12.
9. **Submission materials updated.** Highlights and cover letter now foreground the information-quality measurement contribution and the new sensitivity check.
